Help & support.

Something not working, or an idea for Blurb? Here's how to reach us.

Email support@blurb.fyi

The quickest way to get help, including private and privacy questions. Please include your Blurb version (Settings → About Blurb), your phone model and Android version.

Email support

Before you write, the troubleshooting guide covers the most common problems.

Report a bug or suggest a feature

With a GitHub account, you can also post publicly and follow progress.

Report a bugTell us what happened and what you expected.Suggest a featureShare a problem you would like Blurb to solve.Known issuesBrowse reports and follow their progress.
GitHub reports are public. Do not include private messages, API keys, purchase tokens, receipts or personal details. Use fictional examples and check screenshots before sharing.

Update Blurb

Blurb updates through Google Play. Open the Play Store, search for Blurb and tap Update. When Blurb shows An update is available, tap it to go straight to the listing.

Purchases

For Pro, try Restore purchase in Settings → Blurb Pro using the Google Play account that bought it. Pro is a one-time unlock; cloud API charges are separate. See the Pro guide for preview and payment details.

Summaries stop in the background

If summaries stop when Blurb is closed, use the background battery note or Settings → Background activity. See troubleshooting for phone-specific guidance.
