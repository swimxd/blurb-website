Blurb turns long message notifications into short summaries, so you can decide what needs your attention.

Summarize on your phone with a downloaded model, or use a cloud provider. You choose the apps to monitor.

Start free. Unlock more apps when you need them.

Google Messages summaries are free and unlimited. Try 10 successful summaries shared across Signal, Discord, Microsoft Teams, WhatsApp and Telegram, then unlock those apps with Blurb Pro.

Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play, with no Blurb subscription. The app shows your local price before checkout. Cloud provider API charges are separate; on-device processing uses a downloaded model.

A little help staying ready

Choose cloud processing during setup and Blurb offers a one-time suggestion to explore on-device fallback. A small background battery note helps you find the phone settings that keep summaries running. Original messages on expand starts off; you can enable it in Advanced settings.
